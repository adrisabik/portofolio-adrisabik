## The Context

eMuslim is a comprehensive Muslim encyclopedia app designed to guide users in both their relationship with God (*Habluminallah*) and with other humans (*Habluminannas*).

## The Challenge

Building a reliable prayer time and Qibla direction feature that works accurately across different locations and time zones, while maintaining a smooth offline experience.

## The Engineering Solution

Implemented location-based services using GPS and network triangulation, with offline caching for prayer times and Qibla calculations.

## The Result

Successfully deployed app serving the Muslim community with accurate prayer schedules and comprehensive religious guidance.
